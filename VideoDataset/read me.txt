Condition and terms to use the WhampoaScene dataset

1) please acknowledge one of the following papers if you are using our database in your publications:

Y.-L. Hou and G. K. H. Pang, "Automated people counting at a mass site," in IEEE International Conference on Automation and Logistics, 2008, pp. 464-469.

Y.-L. Hou and G. K. H. Pang, "People Counting and Human Detection in a
Challenging Situation," IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man and Cybernetics,
Part A: Systems and Humans, vol. 41, pp. 24-33, 2010.


2) please do not distribute the database to others without the 
authorization from Dr. Grantham Pang.

Description of the dataset:
1) The dataset was taken in a public event.During around four hours, one image was saved every 100 seconds. The filenames indicate the exact time when each image was saved. 

2) The groundtruth were obtained by mannual annotation.
